# Exception ADR

An approved, time-limited deviation from the [architecture principles](../standards/architecture-principles.md).

- Required for **any** deviation from the principles ([ADR Process](../standards/adr-process.md)).
- Approved by the [ARB](../entities/arb.md): weekly review (Thursdays), two approvals.
- **Expires after 12 months** — must be renewed or resolved.
- Lives in the team's own repo under `docs/adr/`.
