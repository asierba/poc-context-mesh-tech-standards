# ARB (Architecture Review Board)

Platform leads plus one rotating engineer from each domain ([Company-wide Glossary](../synthesis/company-glossary.md)).

- Approves ADRs, including [exception ADRs](../concepts/exception-adr.md) — weekly review on Thursdays, two approvals needed ([ADR Process](../standards/adr-process.md)).
- Owns the tech radar; reviews it quarterly ([Tech Radar](../standards/tech-radar-2026-q3.md)).
